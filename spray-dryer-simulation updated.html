Operating window

Green cells: moisture 6.8 to 7.8% and both checked size classes between 15 and 40%, at the current viscosity, density and nozzle quantity. The dot is the current setting.

Calibrated on 682 plant readings (hot air 635 to 697 °C, 10 to 13 bar, 20 to 28 nozzles, 2.0 to 3.0 °E, 1.610 to 1.666 g/cc). The plant holds moisture near 7.4% by adjusting hot air, so the data alone cannot separate every effect. Moisture follows a drying balance between the slip flow (evaporation load) and the heat from the hot air. It is calibrated so that +5 °C of hot air lowers the moisture by about 0.1%, and +0.5 bar of pump pressure raises it by about 0.17% at the same hot air, as seen in plant operation. This needs the slip flow to rise faster than the nozzle law with pressure, and it gives about 6 °C of hot air per extra nozzle, which is higher than the plant data suggest. Outside the plant range the values are extrapolations. Powder capacity is anchored at 24,500 kg/h (plant range 24,000 to 25,000) for 26 nozzles, 11 bar, 2.4 to 2.8 °E and 1.630 to 1.645 g/cc.
